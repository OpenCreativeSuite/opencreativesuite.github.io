02 — The deal

We're not renting
you software.

The creative industry runs on subscriptions that expire the moment you stop paying. We think that's a strange way to treat the things you make.

01

Free is the feature

No trials, no tiers, no “pro” upsell hiding the export button. Every tool is fully featured on day one — because the work is the point.

02

Your files are yours

Open formats, documented specs and no cloud requirement. Export everything, take it anywhere, open it in ten years.

03

Source in the open

Apache 2.0 with the Commons Clause. Read the code, audit it, fork it for yourself — just don't resell it. The roadmap lives in public.

04

No account. No telemetry.

Download and work offline. We don't know who you are, what you're making, or how many machines you're on — and we're keeping it that way.
