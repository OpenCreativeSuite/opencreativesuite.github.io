01 / Inside Hieroglyph.NET

What you
get.

Every feature ships in the free build. No pro tier, no unlock key, no “contact sales” for the thing you actually need.

01 / 06

A brush that draws

Drag freely and the stroke becomes one smooth, editable outline, with an adjustable width in font units. The pen is there when you want exact curves.

02 / 06

Guides that mean something

Baseline, x-height, cap height, ascender and descender on the canvas, with sidebearing shading and a draggable advance width.

03 / 06

Space the alphabet

Advance widths and sidebearings for every character, plus a visual kerning editor for AV, To, Ta and any pair you choose.

04 / 06

See it as text

Live text, the full alphabet and kerning previews while you edit, and a specimen sheet that presents the finished family.

05 / 06

Real font files

TrueType, CFF, kerning tables and web-font containers compiled from your outlines, with a report written for people rather than compilers.

06 / 06

Yours to keep

Projects are readable .hgl JSON files. Export TTF, OTF, WOFF or WOFF2, install on Windows in one click, and own the typeface outright.

02 / A look inside

The workspace.

Fast, quiet and built around the work. Dark interface, honest controls, and a canvas that gets out of the way.
