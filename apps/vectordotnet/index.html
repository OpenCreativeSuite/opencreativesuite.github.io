01 / Inside VectorDotNet

What you
get.

Every feature ships in the free build. No pro tier, no unlock key, no “contact sales” for the thing you actually need.

01 / 06

Draw real curves

A pen with cubic anchors and mirrored handles, a smoothing pencil, lasso and full direct-selection editing.

02 / 06

Live shapes

Rectangles, ellipses, stars, polygons and spirals keep their parameters. Change a corner radius or add points later.

03 / 06

Snap to anything

Smart guides for object edges and centres, artboards, the grid and dragged ruler guides, with Shift to constrain.

04 / 06

Documents, organised

Nested groups, unlimited layers, locks, visibility, artboards, tabs and independent undo histories.

05 / 06

Colour and type

An HSV picker with alpha and document palettes; searchable system fonts with live previews and runtime font loading.

06 / 06

Clean SVG in and out

Import SVG and SVGZ into editable groups and paths. Export standards-compliant SVG or SVGZ, not a flattened picture.

02 / A look inside

The workspace.

Fast, quiet and built around the work. Dark interface, honest controls, and a canvas that gets out of the way.
